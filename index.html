Birds of the World

Birds are fascinating creatures found across every continent. This page highlights some of the most well-known and visually striking birds, along with facts about their behavior, habitat, and appearance.

Robin


Robins are recognizable by their reddish-orange chests. They are often associated with spring and are commonly seen in gardens and parks. Robins are territorial and sing melodious tunes.

Blue Jay


Blue Jays are intelligent and social birds known for their bold blue feathers and loud calls. They are native to North America and are part of the crow family. Blue Jays are also known to mimic other birds.

Hummingbird


Hummingbirds are among the smallest birds and are famous for their ability to hover and fly backwards. They feed on nectar and can flap their wings up to 80 times per second. Their bright colors and speed make them fascinating to observe.
